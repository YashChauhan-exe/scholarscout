Behind the Scenes

Built for THINK BEYOND 2026. A transparent look at our architecture, matching engine, and privacy-first approach.

The Core Philosophy

Millions of students miss out on government schemes because the information is scattered, wrapped in bureaucratic jargon, and locked behind mandatory accounts. ScholarScout reverses this model. We ask simple questions, run them against a centralized database, and deliver plain-language, actionable results in milliseconds.

Tech Stack

  • Frontend: HTML5, Vanilla JavaScript, and Tailwind CSS for a responsive, lightweight UI without heavy frameworks.
  • Matching engine: Plain JavaScript that runs in the browser against a structured rule file (schemes.json), so your answers never leave your device.
  • Hosting: Netlify (free tier) with automatic deployment from GitHub.

Zero-Data Privacy

We believe eligibility checks should not cost you your personal data.

  • No login walls or mandatory account creation.
  • No phone numbers, emails, or Aadhaar required.
  • Your answers are processed in your browser and never sent to a server.

The Matching Engine
